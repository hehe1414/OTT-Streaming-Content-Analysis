
# Netflix Content Strategy Memo

## Project: OTT Streaming Content Trend Analysis

### Overview
I analysed the Netflix titles dataset containing 8,807 records
and 12 columns using Python, Pandas and data visualisation.

The analysis focused on content types, popular genres,
release trends, countries, ratings and movie durations.

### Key Findings

1. Movies vs TV Shows
Movies account for approximately 69.6% of the catalogue,
while TV Shows account for 30.4%. This shows that movies
make up the larger share of Netflix content.

2. Popular Genres
International Movies is the most common genre, followed
by Dramas and Comedies. This indicates strong representation
of international and drama-based content.

3. Content Release Trends
The year 2018 recorded the highest number of titles
in the dataset, with 1,147 releases. Content additions
increased considerably in the years before that peak.

### Additional Observations

- The United States contributes the highest number
  of titles, followed by India and the United Kingdom.
- TV-MA is the most common content rating.
- The average movie duration is approximately 99.58 minutes.

### Recommendations

1. Continue exploring international content, especially
   drama and comedy categories.
2. Consider expanding regional content opportunities
   in markets such as India.
3. Maintain a balanced catalogue of movies and TV shows
   to serve different audience preferences.

### Conclusion

This analysis helped me understand how data can be used
to identify content patterns and support business decisions.
It also gave me practical experience in data cleaning,
exploratory data analysis, SQL and visualisation.
